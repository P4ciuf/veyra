# Veyra Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make the Veyra community welcoming, safe, inclusive, and respectful for everyone.

We are committed to fostering an environment that respects the dignity, rights, and contributions of all individuals, regardless of race, ethnicity, caste, color, age, physical characteristics, neurodiversity, disability, sex or gender, gender identity or expression, sexual orientation, language, philosophy or religion, national or social origin, socio-economic position, level of education, or any other status.

Everyone who participates in the Veyra community in good faith and follows this Code of Conduct is entitled to the same opportunities and privileges.

## Encouraged Behaviors

We expect all community members to contribute positively to the Veyra ecosystem and help maintain a constructive environment.

The following behaviors are encouraged:

1. **Respect:** Respect the purpose of the community, its activities, and its members.
2. **Kindness:** Communicate honestly, politely, and constructively.
3. **Open-mindedness:** Respect different viewpoints, experiences, and perspectives.
4. **Responsibility:** Take responsibility for your actions and contributions.
5. **Constructive Feedback:** Give and receive feedback professionally and respectfully.
6. **Accountability:** Acknowledge mistakes and make reasonable efforts to address any harm caused.
7. **Community Well-being:** Act in ways that promote a healthy, welcoming, and collaborative environment.

## Restricted Behaviors

The following behaviors are not tolerated within the Veyra community. This includes participating in, threatening, encouraging, or promoting such behavior.

1. **Harassment:** Unwanted personal attention, intimidation, or continued interaction after someone has clearly requested it to stop.
2. **Personal Attacks:** Insulting, demeaning, threatening, or deliberately humiliating other community members.
3. **Discrimination:** Discriminatory behavior, stereotyping, or exclusion based on personal characteristics or identity.
4. **Inappropriate Conduct:** Sexualized, exploitative, or otherwise inappropriate behavior within the context of the community.
5. **Privacy Violations:** Sharing personal, confidential, or private information without the individual's permission.
6. **Endangerment:** Threatening, encouraging, or engaging in violence or other harmful activities against individuals or groups.
7. **Disruptive Behavior:** Any other conduct that significantly compromises the safety, trust, or well-being of the community.

### Additional Restrictions

The following behaviors are also prohibited:

1. **Impersonation:** Pretending to be another individual, project representative, or community member, including attempts to evade moderation actions.
2. **Missing Attribution:** Failing to properly credit original authors or sources when using or contributing external content.
3. **Unsolicited Promotion:** Posting advertisements, spam, or commercial content outside the community's accepted guidelines.
4. **Irresponsible Content Sharing:** Sharing content involving restricted behaviors without appropriate context, warnings, or consideration for other community members.

## Reporting an Issue

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this Code of Conduct, we encourage you to report it to the Veyra moderation team.

Reports can be submitted through the following channels:

- **Official Discord Server:** Contact the moderation team or open a support ticket through the appropriate channel.
- **Project Maintainers:** Contact the Veyra maintainers through the available official communication channels.

### What to Include in a Report

To help the moderation team investigate an incident, please provide as much relevant information as possible:

- **Description:** A clear explanation of what happened.
- **People Involved:** The individuals involved, if known.
- **Context:** Relevant circumstances surrounding the incident.
- **Evidence:** Screenshots, message links, or other relevant documentation.
- **Additional Information:** Any details that may help the investigation.

### Investigation Process

All reports will be reviewed by the Veyra moderation team or authorized project maintainers.

Depending on the situation, the investigation may involve reviewing relevant messages, logs, available evidence, or speaking with the individuals involved.

Reports will be handled as fairly and confidentially as possible. Information will only be shared with individuals who need it to investigate or resolve the situation.

Enforcement decisions will generally be communicated privately to the individuals involved.

## Enforcement Guidelines

Project maintainers and authorized community moderators are responsible for enforcing this Code of Conduct.

Violations will be evaluated based on their severity, frequency, impact on affected individuals, and any previous incidents.

Depending on the circumstances, the following actions may be taken:

### 1. Warning

A private, written warning explaining the violation and why the behavior is unacceptable.

The individual may be asked to acknowledge their actions, apologize, or clarify their understanding of community expectations.

### 2. Temporary Restrictions

Temporary limitations on participation in specific community spaces, channels, or activities.

This measure may be used for repeated minor violations or an initial incident of greater severity.

### 3. Temporary Suspension

Temporary removal from the Veyra community or relevant services.

Suspensions may include specific conditions that must be met before participation can be restored.

### 4. Permanent Ban

Permanent removal from the Veyra community and, where appropriate, restriction from accessing relevant Veyra services and platforms.

This measure may be applied in cases of repeated violations or particularly serious misconduct.

### Enforcement Discretion

The enforcement measures listed above represent general guidelines and do not require moderators to follow a fixed sequence.

Depending on the severity and circumstances of an incident:

- Multiple warnings may be issued before stronger measures are taken.
- Certain restrictions may be applied immediately.
- Serious violations may result in immediate suspension or permanent removal without prior warning.

All enforcement decisions are made at the discretion of the project maintainers or authorized moderators, with the primary objective of protecting the community.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all Veyra community spaces, including:

- Official Discord servers and communication channels.
- Veyra repositories and project discussions.
- Community contributions and interactions.
- Other platforms officially managed by Veyra.

It also applies when an individual is officially representing Veyra in public or external spaces.

Examples include using an official Veyra email address, publishing content through official social media accounts, or representing the project at online or offline events.
